Keep the broadcasts you choose.

YouTube Live Archiver records a user-selected YouTube video or livestream on GitHub Actions and saves the recording to that user's Google Drive.

This site explains the app and its data practices. The recorder runs in the maintainer's private repository; this public site contains no credentials, recordings, or recording controls.

Choose a video

The user starts a recording with a YouTube URL. The app checks video availability and Drive access before recording.

Save as it runs

During normal live capture, completed safety parts are uploaded while the broadcast continues.

Keep the result

When capture ends, the app joins the parts where possible and verifies the uploaded recording's checksum.

Limited Google Drive access

The app requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It uses this authorization to create and manage its archive folder and recording files. It does not request permission to read the user's entire Drive.

Google authorization is required before recordings can be saved. Access can be revoked at any time in the user's Google Account settings.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about access, storage, sharing, retention, and revocation, and the Terms of Use before using the tool.
