Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 October 2026. YouTube Live Archiver is a personal tool maintained by royal-cloud for recording selected videos or livestreams and saving them to the authorized user's Google Drive.

Use content you are authorized to record

The user is responsible for choosing content they are permitted to access, record, and retain, and for complying with applicable law and the terms of YouTube, Google, and GitHub. This tool does not grant rights to third-party content.

Authorization and account control

The user must authorize Google Drive access and controls which videos are selected and when workflows run. The app requests only the Drive file permission described in the Privacy Policy. Authorization can be revoked in Google Account settings.

Availability and limits

Recording depends on stream availability, YouTube access from GitHub's network, Google Drive storage, authorization validity, and GitHub Actions limits. Capture may fail, be interrupted, contain gaps, or remain available only as separate parts. A normal run has a recording time budget of five hours, including any waiting time, with additional job time reserved for uploading.

The tool is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted service or complete recordings. The user should check the workflow result and stored files before relying on an archive.

Costs and storage

The user is responsible for any GitHub Actions usage charges and storage charges under their own service plans. The tool does not collect payments or create a paid subscription.

Changes and support

The maintainer may update or discontinue the personal tool. Information about its current behavior is available here and in the private recorder's documentation. For questions, use the public information repository; never post secrets or cookies.
